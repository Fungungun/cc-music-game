AMEB piano practice between lessons

Use this one-page plan when a student needs short, focused support with theory, rhythm, aural work and general knowledge between piano lessons.

Start here Grade 1 free

Grade 2–3 practice is optional and unlocks once for AUD 14.99 if the student needs it later.

What to practise first

Note values and rests

Short rhythm-value questions before written work becomes a guessing exercise.

Aural listening

Higher/lower pitch, steady beat and simple listening words for five minutes at a time.

Score questions

Key, time signature, terms, signs and form from the student’s own piece.

A simple week

  • Day 1Try the free Grade 1 note-values practice for 10 minutes.
  • Day 2Tap the beat while playing or listening to one current piece.
  • Day 3Answer one score question: key, time signature, sign, term or form.
  • Day 4Do one short aural task: higher/lower, smooth/detached or loud/soft.
  • Day 5Repeat the weakest topic and write one question for the next lesson.
Independent resource: Music Maestro is not affiliated with or endorsed by AMEB. Use your teacher and current syllabus first. The free material is designed to support between-lesson practice, not replace lessons.
