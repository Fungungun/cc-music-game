Aural Training

Pitch, intervals and beat — all by ear!
How to Play ▼

Higher or Lower — hear two notes and say whether the second is higher or lower (this is the Grade 1 exam task!).

Intervals — hear two notes and name the interval. Press ▶ as many times as you like; after 2 wrong answers the notes are revealed.

Tap the Beat — after a 4-count lead-in, tap the big button in time with the music, just like clapping the beat in the exam.
